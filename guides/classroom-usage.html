Schools

Classroom usage guide

This is the day-to-day guide for teachers and students. It covers Mathwise, Knowledge Hub, and Socratic Dialogue the way people actually use them after the school IT person has signed everyone in.

If you are setting up the stack, OIDC, or backups, that lives in the ops docs. This page assumes you can already open the school portal and land inside an app.

Start here

Thoughtlane is one sign-in that opens a few classroom apps. You pick an app from the portal home page. You can jump between apps; your school account follows you.

  • Mathwise for worksheets, step-by-step solutions, and guided practice.
  • Knowledge Hub for the school library, cited search, quizzes, and courses.
  • Socratic Dialogue for level-based lessons where students write answers in their own words.

Classes and sections are shared. When a teacher assigns work to “Period 3,” that same section shows up wherever the product needs a class list. Students do not manage that. Teachers usually do not either, unless your school gave them permission. Rosters are maintained under Administration → Classes.

Tip: In every classroom app there is a Help button in the header. It walks the screen you are on. Use it once on a new tab. It saves a lot of “where did that button go” moments.

Who sees what

What you see depends on your role. Schools map emails to roles like student, teacher, or admin. If a tab is missing, it is almost always permissions, not a bug.

RoleTypical access
Student My assignments / Assignments, practice, lessons, Study (private). Not class-wide assign screens.
Teacher Upload and organize materials, assign to a section, review progress, publish lessons, course assignments.
School admin Users, roles, Classes, External drives, Announcements, and usually Analytics.

Teachers Students Both can open the portal. Teachers get more tabs inside each product.

Home portal

After sign-in you land on the Thoughtlane home page. Cards open each app. There is also Management (Administration) and Analytics if your role allows them.

Students also see a Continue strip and Due this week list when they have open work. Example: Continue opens “Grade 9 Algebra — Week 3 Linear equations” in Mathwise Cognitive Booster; Due this week might list that same practice plus “Fractions reading quiz” (Knowledge Hub) due Friday. Open Class calendar for a printable month or list of deadlines and school events (paste emoji marks on days if you like). Teachers Admins can Add event for the whole school, a grade, or a section (teachers: sections they teach). Students see school-wide events plus those for their grade or section.

Content Manager appears on the portal under School tools as an optional ops browser for school drives. Teachers still use Open from drive inside each product once an admin has registered the drive under Administration → External drives.

Thoughtlane sits beside your LMS (Canvas, Moodle, and so on). Pilots use school SSO and Administration → Classes. LTI launch is prepared under Administration → Integrations and stays off until you enable it after the first pilots.

One-time school setup (someone has to do this first)

Teachers cannot assign to a section that does not exist. Before the first real class:

  1. An admin opens Administration → Classes.
  2. Create a class such as Grade 9 Algebra, then add sections (for example Period 1, Period 2).
  3. Set the section teacher email.
  4. Enroll student emails on each section.

Optional but useful: register school drives under External drives, and post a short welcome notice under Announcements so it shows up as a banner in the apps.

If Assign to class shows “No sections,” the roster is empty or you are not listed as the section teacher. Fix that in Administration before retrying.

For teachers (the loops that matter)

Most weeks you will do one of three things: assign math practice, assign a Hub course, or publish a Socratic lesson. You do not need every product every week.

Mid-week, open Administration → Weekly review to see who has not finished “Week 3 Linear equations” or who is stuck mid-practice. Use Progress board on Period 1 to compare Hub / Mathwise / Socratic status in one grid. (Email or Slack digests are planned later; the tab is the review surface for now.)

Assign math practice for a section

  1. Open Mathwise.
  2. Upload a worksheet (image or PDF), open one from File Manager, or use Open from drive.
  3. Let extraction finish so questions appear. When it completes, a Assign to class shortcut appears for teachers.
  4. Go to Assign to class (or use that shortcut).
  5. Title students will recognize, for example Grade 9 Algebra — Week 3 Linear equations.
  6. Pick the worksheet by name from the dropdown (for example “linear-eq-worksheet.pdf”).
  7. Check one or more sections (Period 1 and Period 2), or use All my sections.
  8. Due defaults to Friday 5:00 PM; change it if you need another deadline.
  9. Optional: Copy last week to reuse the previous worksheet and title pattern, then tweak the week number.
  10. Assign. Later, open Progress on that assignment to see who finished Cognitive questions.

File Manager is for files and folders. Assign to class is for sending practice to people. Mixing those two up is the most common teacher confusion, so we say it plainly here.

Assign a Knowledge Hub course

  1. Make sure documents are uploaded and indexed.
  2. Generate or review quiz items, then publish a course such as Grade 9 Algebra — Linear equations reading.
  3. Open Course assignments.
  4. Pick the published course, a learner or a whole section (Period 1), due window, attempt limits.
  5. Assign. Students find it under My assignments in Knowledge Hub (and it also shows up in the shared inbox other apps can read).

Publish a Socratic lesson

  1. On Grades, sync from Management once so people match your Classes list.
  2. On Courses, import or open the course catalog. If you import, wait for the job to finish, then save only the courses you want. You do not have to keep every extracted line.
  3. Attach materials under Your files for that course.
  4. Use Make a lesson, edit the plan (for example “Discuss slope from a graph”), publish.
  5. Set Who it’s for (grade to level mapping).
  6. On the Dashboard, approve suggested scores when students finish.

Catalog import and “Make a lesson” keep running if you reload the page. Watch the job strip at the top. You can cancel if you started the wrong file.

Learning paths (optional)

In Knowledge Hub, create a path such as Grade 9 Algebra — Linear equations unit: Hub course “Linear equations reading” → Mathwise worksheet “linear-eq-worksheet.pdf” → Socratic lesson “Discuss slope from a graph”. Pick each step by name (leave None until that piece exists). Teachers can click Assign path to class to push every step into the student inbox for Period 1 / Period 2 in one action.

For students

You mostly open work that was assigned to you. You do not need to know how teachers set sections up.

Finding your work

  • Portal home — Continue / Due this week for the next thing (for example Week 3 Linear equations).
  • Mathwise → My assignments for Cognitive practice and other inbox items.
  • Knowledge Hub → My assignments for courses and quizzes assigned to you.
  • Socratic Dialogue → Assignments for a mixed inbox (math, Hub, lessons).
  • Socratic Dialogue → Lessons for dialogue work your teacher published to your grade/level.

If the same assignment shows up in more than one place, that is normal. The shared inbox is trying to give you one place to look, while each product also keeps its own list.

Doing Cognitive practice (Mathwise)

  1. Open the assignment.
  2. Pick a question.
  3. Write one step at a time in the work area.
  4. Use Hint when you are stuck. It should give the next step, not dump the whole answer.
  5. Check as you go. Progress is saved when you come back later.

Doing a Hub course

  1. Open the course from My assignments.
  2. Take the quizzes or practice tags your teacher included.
  3. Watch attempt limits and due dates. If you are locked out, talk to your teacher; the software will not invent extra attempts.

Socratic lessons

  1. Read the concept material for the level.
  2. Answer in your own words. Paste is discouraged on purpose.
  3. Finish the level. Your mark may wait until a teacher approves it.
  4. Study is private practice from your own notes. Teachers do not see Study sets.

Search in Knowledge Hub

Ask a question in Search when you need help from school materials. Read the citations. If the answer looks off, open the source passage. The point of Hub search is that you can check where it came from.

Upload and extract

Drop a PNG, JPG, or PDF. Wait for questions to appear. If a page is messy (scans, handwriting, weird layouts), try re-extract from File Manager. Alternate PDF extractors show up in advanced mode if your school turned that on.

New uploads keep the original file name in the list. Older files that were stored under long id-looking names may still show that way until you re-upload. Internally the system still uses ids. You should not have to type them.

Solve vs practice

Solve All (or solve one question) is for getting a worked solution with clear steps you can review and export. Cognitive Booster is for students writing steps themselves. Same worksheet can feed both.

File Manager habits that help

  • Put contest or unit sheets in folders with names people understand (for example PascalContest).
  • Use search and sort when the list gets long.
  • Open from drive when the school already has the PDF in Google Drive, OneDrive, or S3.
  • ReExtract if the question list looks incomplete after a first pass.

Assign to class (teachers)

Fill title, worksheet name, section, optional due. Refresh roster if the section list looks stale. Progress is per student Cognitive outcomes, not a gradebook for Solve All.

Teacher workflow that usually works

  1. Upload curriculum PDFs under Documents. Wait until indexing finishes before you rely on Search.
  2. Generate quiz items from a document, then open Review and clean anything weird.
  3. Publish only when the bank looks classroom-ready.
  4. Build a course from those tags, publish the course, then assign it.

Students should not meet raw generated items. Review exists for a reason.

Search tips

  • Ask in plain language about what the school uploaded.
  • Always glance at the cited passage.
  • If Search is empty or nonsense, indexing may still be running, or the doc never made it into the library.

Socratic Dialogue in depth

Teacher side

Think of it as: course exists → files attached → lesson generated and edited → published → students mapped by grade/level → you approve scores.

  • Courses: import catalogs carefully. Save selected courses. Reopen drafts if you left mid-import.
  • Your files: materials for that course. Open from drive works here too.
  • Lesson plan: edit levels and questions before students see them.
  • Who it’s for: which grade hits which level.
  • Dashboard: progress and score approval.
  • Grades / People: sync from Management so you are not maintaining two rosters.
  • Assignments: inbox that can include Mathwise and Hub work.

Student side

  • Lessons: the main path.
  • Assignments: open linked work in other products.
  • Study: private. Good for extra practice without teacher eyes on it.

Optional Hear lesson / Hear question only run when the student asks. Nothing should autoplay at them.

School drives

Admins register connectors (Google Drive, OneDrive, S3, and similar) under Administration. Teachers then use Open from drive inside Mathwise, Knowledge Hub, or Socratic Dialogue. The product keeps a reference and fetches the file when it needs to process it. This is not a live shared disk between products, and it is not “copy everything into Knowledge Hub forever.”

If Open from drive fails for everyone, Content Manager is probably not running, or the connector was never registered. That is an IT fix, not something students can solve.

Announcements

School notices are scheduled in Administration. When one is active for a product, you see a banner at the top. Dismiss if you have read it; new notices can still appear later. You can leave targeting open to everyone, or narrow by role, class tags, grade (for example Grade 9), and/or section (for example Grade 9 Algebra / Period 1). Students and teachers tied to that class or section see the banner; others do not.

Help, accessibility, and feedback

  • Help opens a coach for the current screen. In the first week you may see a soft tip inviting you to open Help; dismiss anytime.
  • Status messages stay on screen. Important results are not sound-only.
  • Use Feedback in the header when something is wrong or confusing. Replies show under your name when support answers.
  • Skip links jump to main content for keyboard users.

If something breaks

I do not see Assign to class

Your account is probably missing teacher permissions. Ask an admin to check roles.

Section dropdown is empty or says Forbidden

Roster issue. Confirm the section exists, has a teacher email that matches you, and that students are enrolled. Hit Refresh roster after the admin saves.

Students do not see the assignment

Check that you assigned the right section, that their email is enrolled there, and that they are looking in My assignments / Assignments (not File Manager). Due dates do not hide the row in most cases, but expired attempt limits can block taking a Hub quiz again.

Extraction or lesson generation seems stuck

In Socratic Dialogue, catalog import and Make a lesson are background jobs. Reload and look at the job strip; use Cancel if needed. Mathwise extraction progress is more tied to the open page today, so keep that tab open until questions appear, or re-run from File Manager.

Search cannot find a document I just uploaded

Wait for indexing. Large PDFs take longer. Confirm the file is in Documents and finished processing.

Open from drive errors

Admin: confirm External drives entry and that Content Manager is up. Teacher: try another file; if every file fails, escalate to IT.

I still see weird id-looking file names in Mathwise

Those are older uploads from before friendly names. Re-upload the PDF, or live with the old label on that one file. New uploads should show the real name.

Still stuck? Use in-app Feedback, or email info@thoughtlane.com with the school name, the app, and what you clicked. Screenshots help.
