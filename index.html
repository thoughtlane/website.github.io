What we do

Classroom tools that stay with the teacher.

Schools use one sign-in and one class list. Teachers assign Mathwise practice, library work, and Adaptive Classroom lessons by name. Students open what is due. Suggested scores wait until someone on staff releases them.

We are not trying to replace your gradebook or timetable. Think of us as the place students work the assignment with help, and teachers still own the mark.

For companies, Docket keeps large document sets searchable and chat-ready against materials you approve. Same habit: your sources, not a random model guess.
