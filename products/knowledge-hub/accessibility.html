Accessibility & Help

Knowledge Hub follows the same Thoughtlane patterns as Mathwise and Socratic Dialogue.

  • Skip link to main content; labelled banner and navigation.
  • Clear dialogs, status messages, and keyboard-friendly controls across Documents, Search, and Practice.
  • In-app Help coach: guided steps on the screen you’re on, optional speech, Escape to exit.
  • Header Feedback for bugs and suggestions — replies appear under your name.
  • Thoughtlane themes (paper + lime, or black + lime) with readable primary buttons.
  • Respects reduced-motion preferences on this site and in the app.
