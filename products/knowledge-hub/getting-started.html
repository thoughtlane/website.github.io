Getting started

Add materials, ask with sources, then practice or assign courses.

Staff path

  1. Open Documents. Upload a PDF or Office file. Add tags for grade, class, and subject.
  2. Open Search. Ask a question. Read the answer and open the cited sources to confirm.
  3. On a document, open Quiz & test. Generate multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, or short-answer items. Tag what you want in Practice.
  4. Review wording before learners see the questions.
  5. Open Practice, filter by those tags, and try a run yourself.
  6. In Courses, bundle tags into a course and assign learners with a due window.
  7. Use in-app Help anytime. Send Feedback from the header if something is unclear.

Learner path

  1. Open Search to ask about materials your school has shared — always check the cited sources.
  2. Open My assignments for teacher-assigned work, or Practice when quizzes are available.
  3. Complete the quiz, submit, and review your score. Skills may appear when you earn a perfect run.
