Questions, answered.

What schools ask before they roll out Knowledge Hub.

How is this different from Socratic Dialogue?

Knowledge Hub is your cited library, quiz banks, and course assignments. Socratic Dialogue is level-based lesson conversations with teacher-approved scores. Both share Thoughtlane design for schools.

Do answers invent facts?

Search is grounded in your uploaded materials. Every answer shows the source passages so teachers and students can open the original document and verify.

Who can upload and who can only search?

Roles and permissions follow your school setup. Staff typically manage the library and quizzes; learners search, practice, and complete assignments.

Can we build quizzes without writing every question by hand?

Yes. Open a document’s Quiz & test tools to draft multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, and short-answer items, then review and tag before Practice or Courses.

Can we assign a course to a whole section?

Yes. Course assignments can target a single learner or a Management class section, with due windows and attempt limits. Learners open work from My assignments.

What are learning paths?

Teachers define a sequence that can include a Knowledge Hub course, a Mathwise job, and a Socratic topic. Learners follow the steps as linked product deep-links.

Is there a guided tour?

Use the in-app Help button anytime. It opens on your current screen and walks through the main flows.
