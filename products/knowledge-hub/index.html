Questions, answered.

What schools ask before they roll out the school library.

How is this different from Adaptive Classroom?

The school library is for searching and chatting with school materials. Lessons and class quizzes live in Adaptive Classroom.

Do answers invent facts?

Search is grounded in your uploaded materials. Every answer shows the source passages so teachers and students can open the original document and verify.

Who can upload and who can only search?

Roles follow your school setup. Anyone with access can chat with files the school has shared. Class work is assigned in Adaptive Classroom.

Can we build quizzes without writing every question by hand?

Yes, in Adaptive Classroom → Files. Select the class file, then Multiple choice or Fill-in. Not in the school library.

Can we assign a lesson to a whole section?

Yes. In Adaptive Classroom, open Files, generate the lesson or questions, then Assign to section. Students open it from their lesson, not from the school library.

Where do courses live?

Courses, lesson plans, and class assignment are in Adaptive Classroom. The school library does not host courses or learning paths.

Is there a guided tour?

Use the in-app Help button anytime. It opens on your current screen and walks through the main flows.

Can we post a notice for the whole school?

Yes. Schedule announcements that appear across Thoughtlane products (useful for term starts, maintenance windows, or classroom reminders).
