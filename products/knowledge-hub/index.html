Questions, answered.

What schools ask before they roll out Knowledge Hub.

How is this different from Socratic Dialogue?

Knowledge Hub is for chatting with a file. Upload class files in Socratic Dialogue to make a lesson, multiple choice, or fill-in, then assign the section.

Do answers invent facts?

Search is grounded in your uploaded materials. Every answer shows the source passages so teachers and students can open the original document and verify.

Who can upload and who can only search?

Roles follow your school setup. Anyone with access can chat with files the school has shared. Class work is assigned in Teacher's Assistant.

Can we build quizzes without writing every question by hand?

Yes, in Teacher's Assistant (Socratic Dialogue) → Files. Select the class file, then Multiple choice or Fill-in. Not in Knowledge Hub.

Can we assign a lesson to a whole section?

Yes. In Teacher's Assistant, open Files, generate the lesson or questions, then Assign to section. Students open it from their lesson, not from Knowledge Hub.

Where do courses live?

Courses, lesson plans, and class assignment are in Teacher's Assistant. Knowledge Hub does not host courses or learning paths.

Is there a guided tour?

Use the in-app Help button anytime. It opens on your current screen and walks through the main flows.

Can we post a notice for the whole school?

Yes. Schedule announcements that appear across Thoughtlane products (useful for term starts, maintenance windows, or classroom reminders).
