Guides · Getting started

Getting started

Upload a worksheet, review step-by-step solutions, practice with guided hints, or assign Cognitive work to a class.

Solve a worksheet

  1. Open Mathwise.
  2. Upload an image or PDF of the question sheet.
  3. Wait while questions are extracted into a list.
  4. Solve all questions or pick one at a time.
  5. Review the steps, then export to share when ready.

Practice with Cognitive Booster

  1. Open Cognitive Booster from your saved worksheets.
  2. Select a question. Write one step per line.
  3. Use Hint for the next step only. Check your work as you go.
  4. Ask follow-up questions in chat. Progress saves when you return.

Assign to a class (teachers)

  1. In Administration → Classes, ensure the section exists and has a teacher.
  2. Copy the Cognitive job ID from Cognitive Booster.
  3. Open Assign to class — title, job ID, section, optional due → Assign.
  4. Use Progress on a row to see Cognitive outcomes per student.

My assignments (learners)

  1. Open My assignments.
  2. Choose Open to jump into Cognitive Booster for that job.

School drives

Teachers can use Open from drive on upload or File Manager. Admins configure Google Drive, OneDrive, S3, and other connectors under Administration → External drives. Products keep a drive reference and fetch bytes when needed — not a shared live filesystem.

Announcements

School notices scheduled in Administration → Announcements appear as a banner in Mathwise and other Thoughtlane apps when they are active.

Help

Click Help anytime for a guided tour of the screen you are on — including classroom tabs and Home.
