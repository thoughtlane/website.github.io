Questions, answered.

What educators and learners ask first.

How is Mathwise different from a chatbot?

Mathwise is built for school worksheets: extract questions, show trusted steps, reopen past work, and export — not a one-off chat reply.

What is Cognitive Booster?

Guided practice: you write steps, request next-step hints, check along the way, and track progress — without seeing the full answer up front.

Can we organize many worksheets?

Yes. Keep uploads and past runs in folders by topic, grade, or date, with search when you need an earlier sheet.

Is there in-app help?

Use Help in the header for a guided tour of the screen you are on.
