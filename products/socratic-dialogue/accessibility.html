Guides · Accessibility

Accessibility

Important information is never sound-only. Help, celebrations, and lesson audio always have a text channel.

What we support

  • Skip link to main content; keyboard-friendly navigation and dialogs
  • In-app Help coach on the screen you are on
  • Optional Hear lesson and Hear question, only when the student asks
  • Optional voice shortcuts for submit, next, and navigation
  • Mute speech and chimes; stop audio anytime
  • Thoughtlane themes and respect for reduced motion
