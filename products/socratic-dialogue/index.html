Questions, answered.

What schools and learners ask first.

How is this different from flashcard apps?

Students write answers in their own words on a lesson you published. Suggested scores wait for you. It is not a drill pack with auto-marks by default.

Do teachers see student Study uploads?

No. Study materials and generated sets are private to the learner. Classroom lessons published by teachers remain shared with the course.

Who controls the final grade?

A suggested mark is prepared. You release or change it before the student sees a score.

Do we have to import a whole catalog?

No. Many teachers start from a class file and a section. Catalog import is there if your school wants it; you still choose what goes live.

Can we reach the whole school with a notice?

Yes. Schedule announcements that appear across Thoughtlane products (useful for term starts, maintenance windows, or classroom reminders) without leaving the school portal.

How do Classes and Assignments fit in?

Sections match the school class list. Learners open Mathwise, the school library, and Adaptive Classroom work from the same due list.

Is there a guided tour?

Use the in-app Help button anytime. It opens on your current screen and walks Teacher or Student paths with optional speech.
