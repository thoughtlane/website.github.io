Questions, answered.

What schools and learners ask first.

How is this different from flashcard apps?

Socratic Dialogue builds level-based curriculum with free-text dialogue, rubrics, and teacher approval — not only MCQ drills. Practice questions unlock after the Socratic path.

Do teachers see student Study uploads?

No. Study materials and generated sets are private to the learner. Classroom lessons published by teachers remain shared with the course.

Who controls the final grade?

A suggested mark is prepared; teachers approve or edit on the Dashboard before students see scores.

Does it work with our course catalog?

Yes. Admins import PDF/Word/Excel catalogs; we extract codes and prerequisites, then teachers attach materials and publish lessons to courses.

Is there a guided tour?

Use the in-app Help button anytime. It opens on your current screen and walks Teacher or Student paths with optional speech.
