Getting started

Pick your role and follow the first-time path for your school.

Roles

Use the Admin | Teacher | Student switch in the header when your account allows it.

ModeWhat you can do
AdminSet up grades, people, and the course catalog (import, review, select what to save); sync roster from Management
TeacherUpload materials, build lesson plans, publish, review scores, sync Classes
StudentComplete dialogue lessons, open Assignments inbox, use private Study (only you see Study uploads)

First-time admin

  1. Sign in with an admin role.
  2. Prefer Administration → Classes for the shared school roster, then sync into Socratic Dialogue.
  3. Import your course catalog, watch progress while courses are found, then select which courses to save.
  4. Add teachers and students (or sync), then assign courses.
  5. Teachers can then write and publish lesson plans.

First-time teacher

  1. Open a course and upload the source materials, or use Open from drive to register a school drive file by reference.
  2. Generate a lesson plan, review levels and questions, then save.
  3. Map who the lesson is for (grades ↔ levels).
  4. Publish for students. Preview as a student if you like.
  5. Approve or edit suggested scores on the Dashboard before students see them.
  6. On Grades, use Sync from Management roster so sections match Classes.

First-time student

  1. Open the lesson link from your teacher.
  2. Read the lesson (optional Hear lesson).
  3. Type your answers and submit (paste is discouraged).
  4. Scores appear after your teacher approves them.
  5. Open Assignments for Mathwise or Knowledge Hub work linked from your school.
  6. Track progress from your Dashboard and My progress.

Announcements

School notices scheduled in Administration → Announcements appear as a banner across Socratic Dialogue and other Thoughtlane apps when they are active.

Study (self-serve)

Anyone can open Study to upload their own notes and generate a private practice set. Teachers and classmates cannot see Study materials.
